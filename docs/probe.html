2 · Sharing a JSON file

canShare({ files }) was already confirmed true on this device. The open question is narrower: does iOS tell the page when you back out? Everything about an honest "last backup" stamp depends on the answer, and the page cannot see which you did — so say which you intend by choosing a button, and then do that.

Do the cancel one first. RESOLVED after a cancel is the bad news — it would mean the app cannot tell a real backup from an abandoned one even through the share sheet. Expect no pagehide/pageshow lines around either: the share sheet does not freeze the document, which is itself why a share-based export cannot trip step 9.
